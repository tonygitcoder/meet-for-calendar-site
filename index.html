Meet for Calendar

A small personal macOS utility

Meet for Calendar adds a “Google Meet” option to the Add Video Call menu in Apple Calendar on macOS. When you choose it, the app creates a new Google Meet meeting room in your own Google account and adds its link to the event.

It is a private, personal tool. It is not sold, not distributed through any app store, and not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Only the Google permission needed to create Meet rooms (meetings.space.created). It does not read your calendar, email, contacts, files or other meetings. See the privacy policy.
